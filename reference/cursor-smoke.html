Move the pointer. Every move past 24px spawns an expanding ring whose edge is crumpled by 3-octave fbm noise and slowly rotated, fading over 1.8s. Up to 12 live at once, summed additively over a faint film grain. The noise-wrinkled edge plus the grain is what reads as smoke.

One fullscreen WebGL fragment shader, no simulation and no dependencies. Not the Navier-Stokes fluid solver that most “smoke cursor” libraries wrap.

Ported from the RippleBackground component on vuilabs.ai. Was live on this site until the particle-field background replaced it. Self-suspends when the pointer is still; skipped under prefers-reduced-motion.
